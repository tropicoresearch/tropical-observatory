Tropical Weather & Climate Observatory

Explore tropical precipitation, ocean temperatures, atmospheric convection, and short-range precipitation forecasts through regularly updated scientific maps. The observatory brings together satellite observations and numerical weather prediction products, with a focus on the tropics and the Americas.

Four scheduled updates daily (UTC) · Products reflect the latest available data from each source

About the Observatory

Tropical Weather & Climate Observatory is a scientific visualization and outreach initiative developed by Alejandro Jaramillo-Moreno, Research Scientist at the Instituto de Ciencias de la Atmósfera y Cambio Climático (ICAyCC), Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM), and one of the lead researchers of TRÓPICO — Research Group on Tropical Climatology, Hydrology, and Meteorology.

Observation dates and forecast cycles are displayed with their individual products. Data sources have different availability times, so scheduled updates do not imply that every dataset is refreshed four times a day.

Disclaimer

This is an independent scientific visualization and outreach initiative, not an official operational product of NOAA or UNAM. Its products are intended for research, education, and public communication, not for official weather warnings or hazard-related decisions.

About the author
